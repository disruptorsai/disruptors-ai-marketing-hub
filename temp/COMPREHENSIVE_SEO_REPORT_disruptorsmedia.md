### Risk 1: SPA Crawlability
**Issue:** React SPAs can be harder for Google to crawl
**Mitigation:**
- Implement Netlify pre-rendering for critical pages
- Add static meta tags to index.html
- Consider SSR for service pages

### Risk 2: Content Creation Bandwidth
**Issue:** Writing 40+ blog posts requires significant time
**Mitigation:**
- Use AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT) for first drafts
- Hire freelance writers for $0.10-0.15/word
- Repurpose existing content (turn case studies into blog posts)

### Risk 3: Slow Google Indexing
**Issue:** New content can take 2-4 weeks to rank
**Mitigation:**
- Submit XML sitemap to Google Search Console
- Build backlinks to new content
- Share on social media for faster discovery
- Update existing content regularly (signals freshness)

### Risk 4: Keyword Competition
**Issue:** "AI marketing" space is competitive
**Mitigation:**
- Focus on long-tail keywords first
- Leverage local advantage (Utah-specific keywords)
- Build topical authority before tackling head terms
- Target informational keywords with blog content
