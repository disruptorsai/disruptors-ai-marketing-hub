## Next Steps to Launch

### Phase 1: Design & Format (2-3 hours)

- Add visuals: diagrams, screenshots, workflow charts

**Deliverables:**

- Optional: Notion versions for interactive use

---

### Phase 2: Landing Pages (2 hours)
**Task:** Create lead capture pages for each resource

**Use Your Existing System:**
- `/l/n8n-workflows` → Landing page for workflows
- `/l/chatgpt-prompts` → Landing page for prompts
- `/l/content-workflow` → Landing page for content system

**Each Landing Page:**
- Hero: Problem statement + value prop
- Preview: 3-5 bullet points of what's inside
- Social proof: "Used by 50+ businesses" / data points
- Form: Email + name (Google One Tap signup)
- Redirect: `/g/[slug]` with download link

---

### Phase 3: Automation Setup (1 hour)
**Task:** Connect lead capture to nurture sequences

**Email Sequence (5 emails over 10 days):**
1. **Day 0:** Delivery + quick win
2. **Day 2:** "How's it working?" + specific tip
3. **Day 5:** Case study / testimonial
4. **Day 7:** Related resource (cross-sell other lead magnets)
5. **Day 10:** "Ready for done-for-you?" + booking link

**Tools:**
- ConvertKit / Mailchimp
- Supabase (track downloads via your lead magnet system)
- n8n workflow (optional: auto-track engagement)

---

### Phase 4: Social Media Launch (Ongoing)
**Week 1-3:** Post resources (LinkedIn + Twitter per schedule)
**Week 4:** Results post + testimonials
**Ongoing:** Respond to comments, track metrics

**Metrics to Track:**
- Downloads per resource
- Conversion rate (download → email subscriber)
- Email open rates
- Booking requests from email sequences
- Social engagement (likes, comments, shares)

---

### Phase 5: Optimization (After 30 days)
**Review:**
- Which resource got most downloads?
- Which posts got most engagement?
- Which email in sequence got best open rate?
- What feedback/questions came up?

**Optimize:**
- Double down on winning resource (create "part 2")
- A/B test social posts (different hooks)
- Refine email sequence based on engagement
- Update resources with new trends/data
