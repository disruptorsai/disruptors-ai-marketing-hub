## The Business Brain Formula

Before using ANY prompt, add this to ChatGPT as a custom instruction:

```
You are writing for [COMPANY NAME].

Brand Voice:
- Tone: [friendly/professional/witty/authoritative]
- We serve: [your ideal customer]
- We avoid: [jargon/corporate speak/buzzwords]
- Our unique angle: [what makes you different]

Writing Rules:
1. Lead with value, not features
2. Use real examples and data
3. Keep it scannable (short paragraphs, bullets)
4. Every piece needs ONE clear CTA
5. Sound human - contractions, conversational flow

Example content we love:
[Paste 2-3 sentences from your best-performing content]
```

**This one setup will transform every prompt below from "generic AI slop" to "sounds exactly like your brand."**

## Social Media Prompts (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram)

### 1. LinkedIn Thought Leadership Post
```
Write a LinkedIn post about [TOPIC] that:
- Opens with a controversial or surprising statement
- Shares a recent lesson or mistake I learned
- Includes 1-2 data points or statistics
- Ends with an open-ended question to drive comments
- 1,300 characters max
- Format: Hook paragraph, 3 bullets, closing thought
```

### 2. Twitter Thread That Goes Viral
```
Create a Twitter thread about [TOPIC]:
- Hook tweet: Make a bold claim or share surprising stat
- Tweets 2-4: Break down the concept (one idea per tweet)
- Tweet 5: Real-world example or case study
- Tweet 6: Actionable tip readers can use today
- Tweet 7: CTA + tag 2-3 relevant accounts
- Each tweet: 240 characters max (leave room for RT comments)
```

### 3. Instagram Carousel Post
```
Create an Instagram carousel post about [TOPIC]:
- Slide 1: Bold headline + eye-catching stat (design note: white text, gradient bg)
- Slides 2-8: One tip per slide, numbered
- Slide 9: Recap of all tips
- Slide 10: CTA (comment with favorite tip)
- Caption: 150 words, storytelling opener, end with question
- Hashtags: 10 relevant tags (#YourNiche #SmallBusinessTips etc.)
```

### 4. Behind-the-Scenes Story
```
Write a social media post showing behind-the-scenes of [PROCESS/DAY/CHALLENGE]:
- Start with "Nobody talks about..."
- Share the messy reality
- Include a small win or lesson learned
- Make it relatable (not aspirational)
- End with "Can you relate?" or similar engagement question
```

### 5. Data-Driven Insight Post
```
I have this data: [PASTE DATA]

Create a LinkedIn post that:
- Pulls out 3 surprising insights
- Explains why each matters to [YOUR AUDIENCE]
- Adds context (is this good/bad? trending up/down?)
- Ends with prediction or implication
- Professional but not boring tone
```

## Email Marketing Prompts

### 6. Welcome Email Sequence (5 emails)
```
Create a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to [PRODUCT/SERVICE]:
- Email 1 (Day 0): Welcome + set expectations + quick win
- Email 2 (Day 2): Your origin story (why you started)
- Email 3 (Day 4): Biggest mistake [AUDIENCE] makes + how to avoid it
- Email 4 (Day 7): Case study or testimonial
- Email 5 (Day 10): Special offer + urgency

Each email:
- Subject line (curiosity + benefit)
- 200 words max
- One CTA
- P.S. with social proof or bonus
```

### 7. Re-Engagement Email
```
Write an email to win back inactive subscribers who haven't opened in 60 days:
- Subject: Self-aware ("We miss you" or "Breaking up?")
- Body: Acknowledge the silence, no shame
- Remind them why they subscribed
- Give ONE reason to stay (new content/tool/offer)
- Make unsubscribe easy (show you respect them)
- Tone: Friendly, no desperation
```

### 8. Product Launch Email
```
Write a product launch email for [PRODUCT]:
- Subject: Teasing benefit, not feature
- Hook: Paint the problem (make it visceral)
- Agitate: Why current solutions fail
- Solution: Introduce product as "the better way"
- Proof: 1-2 testimonials or data points
- CTA: Clear next step
- Urgency: Limited time/quantity
- 300 words max
```

### 9. Newsletter Email Template
```
Create a weekly newsletter email template:
- Opening: One-sentence industry update or insight
- Section 1: "What we're working on" (transparency)
- Section 2: "Best of the web" (3 curated links with 1-sentence context)
- Section 3: "Quick tip" (actionable advice, 50 words)
- Closing: Question to drive replies
- No more than 400 words total
```

### 10. Cart Abandonment Email
```
Write a cart abandonment email for someone who left [PRODUCT] in cart:
- Subject: Playful, not pushy ("Forget something?")
- Remind what they left
- Address #1 objection for this product
- Include 1 testimonial addressing that objection
- Offer: 10% discount (expires in 24 hours)
- Make checkout one-click easy
- Tone: Helpful friend, not desperate salesperson
```

## Blog Content Prompts

### 11. SEO Blog Post Outline
```
Create an outline for an SEO blog post targeting keyword: [KEYWORD]

Requirements:
- H1: Question format including keyword
- Introduction: 100 words, hook + preview
- 5-7 H2 sections covering user intent
- Each H2: 300-400 words
- Include H3 subsections where needed
- Every section answers: What? Why? How?
- Conclusion: Summary + CTA
- Suggest 3 internal linking opportunities
- Meta description: 155 characters
```

### 12. Listicle Post
```
Write "10 [X] That [BENEFIT]" about [TOPIC]:
- Intro: Why this matters now (tie to trend/stat)
- Each item: Bolded title + 100-150 word explanation + one actionable tip
- Include mix of obvious + surprising items
- Item #1: Most important (front-load value)
- Item #10: Controversial or unexpected
- Conclusion: Which will you try first?
- Tone: Helpful expert, not know-it-all
```

### 13. Case Study Blog Post
```
Turn these results into a case study blog post:
[PASTE CLIENT RESULTS]

Structure:
- H1: "[X]% increase in [METRIC] in [TIMEFRAME]"
- Section 1: The Challenge (paint the pain)
- Section 2: What They Tried First (and why it failed)
- Section 3: Our Approach (3-step process)
- Section 4: The Results (data viz ideas)
- Section 5: Key Takeaways (what readers can apply)
- CTA: "Want similar results?"
- 1,200 words
```

### 14. "Ultimate Guide" Post
```
Create a comprehensive guide on [TOPIC]:
- Introduction: Why this guide exists (gap in current info)
- Chapter breakdown (6-8 main sections)
- Each chapter: Overview + deep dive + examples + common mistakes
- Throughout: "Pro Tips" callouts
- End: Checklist or step-by-step summary
- Downloadable PDF mention (lead magnet)
- 3,000+ words
- SEO keywords naturally woven in
```

### 15. Comparison Post
```
Write "[X] vs [Y]: Which Is Better for [USE CASE]?"

Format:
- Intro: Why people struggle choosing
- Quick answer upfront (don't make them scroll)
- Feature comparison table (markdown)
- Section 1: When to choose X
- Section 2: When to choose Y
- Section 3: Our recommendation
- Pros/cons of each
- Real user reviews quote
- CTA: Try one with our guide
```

## Ad Copy Prompts

### 16. Facebook Ad Copy (Problem-Aware Audience)
```
Write Facebook ad copy for [PRODUCT] targeting people who know they have [PROBLEM]:
- Headline: Call out the problem (45 characters)
- Body: 3 sentences - problem, agitation, solution tease
- Make them think "How did they know?"
- CTA: "Learn how" or "See how it works"
- Avoid salesy language
- 120 words max
```

### 17. Google Search Ad
```
Create a Google Search ad for keyword: [KEYWORD]
- Headline 1: Include keyword (30 characters)
- Headline 2: Unique value prop (30 characters)
- Headline 3: CTA or offer (30 characters)
- Description 1: Benefit-focused (90 characters)
- Description 2: Social proof + CTA (90 characters)
- All headlines + descriptions should work in any combination
```

### 18. LinkedIn Sponsored Content
```
Write LinkedIn ad for [B2B PRODUCT]:
- Headline: Outcome-focused (match job title's goal)
- Body: Open with stat or industry insight
- Position product as "the shortcut"
- Include subtle authority marker (clients, years, etc.)
- CTA: Soft (guide/webinar, not "buy")
- Tone: Colleague, not vendor
```

### 19. Retargeting Ad (Warm Traffic)
```
Write retargeting ad for people who visited [PAGE] but didn't convert:
- Acknowledge they were looking
- Address likely objection based on page
- Offer: Case study/demo/trial (low friction)
- Urgency: Subtle (not "ACT NOW")
- Include testimonial snippet
- Make next step easy
```

### 20. Video Ad Script (30 seconds)
```
Write a 30-second video ad script for [PRODUCT]:
- Seconds 0-3: Hook (pattern interrupt)
- Seconds 4-10: Problem (show pain)
- Seconds 11-20: Solution (demo product)
- Seconds 21-25: Benefit (outcome)
- Seconds 26-30: CTA + offer
- Visual notes for each scene
- Assume sound ON (most people watch with audio now)
```

## Website Copy Prompts

### 21. Landing Page Hero Section
```
Write hero section for landing page selling [PRODUCT]:
- Headline: Outcome + timeframe ("Get X in Y days")
- Subheading: How it works (10 words)
- 3 benefit bullets (not features)
- CTA button text (action + benefit)
- Trust element (clients/reviews/guarantee)
- Above-fold only, 50 words total
```

### 22. Pricing Page Copy
```
Write pricing page copy with 3 tiers:
- Tier names: Not "Basic/Pro/Enterprise" (creative alternatives)
- Each tier: Name, price, one-liner, 5-7 features
- Most popular tier: Highlighted, "Best value" reasoning
- Address objections: Money-back guarantee, easy cancellation
- FAQ section: 5 pricing questions
- Make expensive tier look like a steal (anchor effect)
```

### 23. About Page Story
```
Write About Us page that doesn't suck:
- Opening: Why we exist (mission, not history)
- The origin story: Make it relatable
- Our approach: What we do differently
- Team section: Bios that show personality
- Values: What we stand for (with examples)
- CTA: How to work with us
- Tone: Confident but not arrogant
- 600 words
```

### 24. Product Description
```
Write ecommerce product description for [PRODUCT]:
- Opening line: Biggest benefit
- Features: List format, each with "so you can..." benefit
- Use case: "Perfect for [SCENARIO]"
- Specs: Table format
- What's included: Bullet list
- Guarantee/return policy
- Reviews summary: "Customers love..."
- 200 words max
```

### 25. FAQ Page
```
Create FAQ page with 15 questions about [PRODUCT/SERVICE]:
- Organize by category (Pricing, Features, Support, etc.)
- Answer each: Short (2-3 sentences) + link to learn more
- Include "questions we wish people asked" (proactive)
- Tone: Friendly, no corporate speak
- Embed video answers for top 5 questions
- End each answer with soft CTA where appropriate
```

## AI Content Creation Prompts

### 26. Content Repurposing
```
I have this long-form content:
[PASTE BLOG POST/VIDEO TRANSCRIPT]

Repurpose into:
- 5 Twitter threads (different angles)
- 3 LinkedIn posts
- 10 Instagram caption ideas
- 5 email subject lines
- 3 TikTok/Reel hooks

Maintain core message but adapt for each platform
```

### 27. Content Calendar
```
Create a 30-day content calendar for [BUSINESS TYPE]:
- Theme for each week
- Post ideas for Mon/Wed/Fri (3x per week)
- Mix of content types: educational, promotional, entertaining, behind-scenes
- Include content format (video/carousel/text/link)
- Suggested hashtags per post
- Note which posts to boost with ads
- Output as table: Date | Topic | Platform | Format | CTA
```

### 28. SEO Keyword Research
```
Suggest 20 blog post topics for [NICHE] that target:
- Long-tail keywords (low competition)
- Question-based searches ("How to...", "What is...", "Why does...")
- For each topic:
  * Primary keyword
  * Monthly search volume estimate
  * User intent (informational/commercial/transactional)
  * Ranking difficulty (easy/medium/hard)
  * Content angle to stand out
```

### 29. Buyer Persona
```
Create detailed buyer persona for [PRODUCT]:
- Name: [First name, job title]
- Demographics: Age, location, income, education
- Psychographics: Values, fears, aspirations
- Daily challenges: Top 5 pain points
- Where they hang out online: Platforms, communities, influencers
- Buying triggers: What makes them finally purchase
- Objections: Why they hesitate
- How they discover solutions: Search terms, referrals
- Language they use: Phrases, jargon
```

### 30. Content Audit Analysis
```
Analyze these URLs and tell me:
[PASTE 5-10 CONTENT URLs]

For each:
- Main topic/keyword targeting
- Content quality (1-10)
- Engagement signals (if visible)
- Improvement opportunities
- Should we: Keep, Update, Delete, or Consolidate?
- Quick win updates (add CTA, improve headline, etc.)

Then: Overall themes, gaps in coverage, consolidation opportunities
```

## Prompt Engineering Tips That Actually Matter

### Make prompts specific:
❌ "Write a blog post about marketing"
✅ "Write a 1,200-word blog post for small business owners explaining how to measure ROI from content marketing, including 3 free tools and one case study. Tone: Helpful expert. Structure: Problem-Solution-Action."

### Give examples:
"Here's an example of our best content: [paste]. Write something similar about [NEW TOPIC]."

### Iterate:
First output not perfect? Use: "Make this more [specific quality]. Remove [what you don't like]. Add more [what's missing]."

### Provide constraints:
- Word count
- Character limits (tweets, meta descriptions)
- Required elements (CTA, stat, example)
- What to avoid

### Ask for multiple versions:
"Give me 5 variations of this headline" often produces 1-2 winners.
