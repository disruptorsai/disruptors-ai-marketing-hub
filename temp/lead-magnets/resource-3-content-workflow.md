## Phase 1: SEED (5 Minutes - You Do This)

### Pick ONE of these seed types:

**Option A: Topic Research**
- Google Trends → What's trending in your niche?
- Reddit/Facebook Groups → What questions keep coming up?
- Your analytics → What content performed best last month?

**Option B: Customer Conversation**
- Recent sales call → What objection did you overcome?
- Support ticket → What problem did you solve?
- Customer win → What result did someone achieve?

**Option C: Industry News**
- Newsletter you read → New data/study/trend?
- Competitor post → What can you add to the conversation?
- Your own experiment → What did you test/learn?

### Write the "Content DNA" doc:

Create a Google Doc or Notion page with:

```markdown
## Content Seed [Date]

**Topic:** [One sentence]

**Audience:** [Who needs this?]

**Hook/Angle:** [Why this matters NOW]

**Key Points:**
1.
2.
3.

**Outcome:** After consuming, audience should be able to [DO WHAT]

**CTA:** [What action do you want them to take?]

**Brand Voice Reminder:**
- Tone: [Your brand's tone]
- Avoid: [Words/phrases you don't use]
- Include: [Your unique terms/frameworks]
```

**Example:**
```markdown
## Content Seed Jan 17, 2025

**Topic:** AI tools wasting small business owners time because they don't have brand context

**Audience:** Small business owners trying to use ChatGPT for marketing

**Hook/Angle:** Everyone's using AI wrong - they treat it like Google instead of an employee who needs onboarding

**Key Points:**
1. Generic AI = generic output (sounds like everyone else)
2. Solution: "Business Brain" - teach AI your brand once, use everywhere
3. Takes 10 mins to set up, saves 10 hours/week

**Outcome:** Readers understand how to "onboard" AI with brand voice

**CTA:** Download our Business Brain template

**Brand Voice Reminder:**
- Tone: Helpful, no-BS, data-driven but friendly
- Avoid: Hype, buzzwords, "revolutionary"
- Include: Real numbers, client examples
```

**Time: 5 minutes** to fill this out. This is your North Star for ALL content this week.

   - "Training AI is technical/complicated" (Actually: 10 min setup)

## Phase 3: CREATE (10 Minutes - AI Does This)

### Generate the "master" long-form content:

**Tool:** Claude 3.5 Sonnet or ChatGPT-4

**The "Business Brain" Prompt:**
```
You are the content writer for [COMPANY NAME].

BRAND VOICE:
[Paste your brand voice from Content DNA]

TASK: Write a LinkedIn article

Topic: [YOUR SEED TOPIC]
Target: [YOUR AUDIENCE]
Research data: [PASTE PHASE 2 RESEARCH]

REQUIREMENTS:
- Length: 1,200 words
- Structure: Hook → Problem → Insight → Solution → Action
- Include: 3 data points from research
- Tone: [YOUR BRAND TONE]
- Add: 1 personal story or client example
- End with: Clear CTA

IMPORTANT RULES:
1. Use "you" (not "one" or "people")
2. Short paragraphs (3-4 lines max)
3. One idea per paragraph
4. Include subheadings for scannability
5. No buzzwords: [YOUR BLACKLIST]
6. Sound like this: [PASTE 2-3 SENTENCES FROM YOUR BEST CONTENT]

Format: Ready to publish, no meta commentary
```

**AI generates:**
- 1,200-word LinkedIn article
- Perfect starting point for everything else

**Save as:** `[topic]-master-content.md`

**Time: 10 minutes** (AI writes, you start review)

## Phase 4: MULTIPLY (5 Minutes - AI Does This)

### Turn master content into 14 platform-specific pieces:

**Tool:** Same AI (Claude/ChatGPT)

**The Multiplication Prompt:**
```
Take this master content and create:

[PASTE MASTER CONTENT]

CREATE:

1. **Twitter Thread (7 tweets)**
   - Hook tweet with stat
   - 5 value tweets (one per key point)
   - CTA tweet
   - Each tweet: 240 chars, conversational

2. **Instagram Carousel (10 slides)**
   - Slide 1: Bold headline + stat
   - Slides 2-9: One tip per slide
   - Slide 10: Recap + CTA
   - Caption: 150 words

3. **Email Newsletter**
   - Subject line options (3)
   - Body: 400 words, sections, one CTA
   - P.S. with bonus link

4. **YouTube Script (5 min video)**
   - Hook (first 10 seconds)
   - Main content (structured)
   - CTA + outro
   - Include: timestamps, B-roll notes

5. **Blog Post SEO Version**
   - Same content, optimized for [KEYWORD]
   - Meta title + description
   - H1, H2s, H3s
   - Internal linking suggestions

6. **LinkedIn Poll**
   - Question about key insight
   - 4 answer options
   - Context paragraph

MAINTAIN:
- Brand voice
- Core message
- Data points
- CTA

Format each section clearly labeled.
```

**AI Output:**
You now have 6 content formats = 14 pieces total (including thread tweets, carousel slides, etc.)

**Save as:** `[topic]-multiplied-content.md`

**Time: 5 minutes** (AI processes while you review master content)

## Phase 5: HUMANIZE (10 Minutes - YOU Do This)

This is the most important phase. AI did the heavy lifting, now you add the magic.

### What to review/add:

**1. Personality Check (2 min)**
- Does it sound like YOU? If not, what's off?
- Add contractions (don't → don't, we are → we're)
- Replace formal phrases with casual ones
- Add your unique expressions/frameworks

**2. Proof & Examples (3 min)**
- Verify data points (AI hallucinates sometimes)
- Add a real client story or personal anecdote
- Replace generic examples with specific ones
- Include timestamps ("Last week, a client...")

**3. Platform Optimization (3 min)**
- **Twitter**: Check character counts, add emojis if brand-appropriate
- **LinkedIn**: Professional but not stiff - remove if AI went too formal
- **Email**: Test subject lines with CoSchedule Headline Analyzer
- **Instagram**: Verify hashtags are relevant + trending

**4. CTA Clarity (2 min)**
- Every piece should have ONE clear next step
- Make it easy (link in comments, DM for link, etc.)
- Match CTA to platform (LinkedIn = download, Twitter = reply, etc.)

### The "Would I Send This?" Test:
- Would you proudly share this with your best client?
- Does it teach something valuable?
- Does it sound like you wrote it?

**If YES to all 3 → Approved**
**If NO to any → Tweak that part**

**Time: 10 minutes total** for all 14 pieces (you're just refining, not rewriting)

TUESDAY:
- 9 AM: Email newsletter

WEDNESDAY:
- 8 AM: Blog post goes live

THURSDAY:
- 10 AM: YouTube video (if applicable)
- 3 PM: Reshare Monday's LinkedIn with new angle

FRIDAY:
- 11 AM: Twitter: "This week we covered..."
- 4 PM: Instagram: Behind-the-scenes/recap

### Quick Scheduling Steps:

1. **Buffer/Later Dashboard:**

3. **Blog:**
   - Upload SEO version
   - Add images/graphics (Canva)
   - Set permalink
   - Schedule publish

4. **Tracking:**
   - Create tracking doc: [URL shortener for each link]
   - Note baseline metrics
   - Set reminder to review Friday

**Time: 5 minutes** (copy-paste, schedule, done)

## Common Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

### ❌ Mistake #1: Skipping Phase 1 (Seed)
**Problem:** AI generates content with no strategic direction
**Fix:** Spend full 5 minutes on Content DNA. It guides everything.

### ❌ Mistake #2: Publishing AI Output Without Review
**Problem:** Generic voice, potential errors, no personality
**Fix:** Phase 5 (Humanize) is NON-NEGOTIABLE. 10 minutes to add YOU.

### ❌ Mistake #3: Using Different Prompts Each Time
**Problem:** Inconsistent voice, reinventing wheel weekly
**Fix:** Save your best prompts as templates. Refine over time.

### ❌ Mistake #4: Not Training AI on Your Brand
**Problem:** Every piece sounds like everyone else's AI content
**Fix:** Create a "Brand Bible" doc (examples of your best content, voice guidelines). Paste into every prompt.

### ❌ Mistake #5: Publishing Same Content Everywhere
**Problem:** Platforms are different; audiences expect different formats
**Fix:** Phase 4 adapts per platform. Trust the multiplied versions.
