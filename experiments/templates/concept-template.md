## Tips for Filling Out This Template

1. **Be Specific**: The more detail you provide, the better the agent can analyze and plan
2. **Include Data**: Current metrics help set baselines and targets
3. **Think User-First**: Focus on user benefits, not just business goals
4. **Define Success Clearly**: What does "working" actually mean?
5. **Be Honest About Constraints**: Budget and timeline reality helps planning
6. **It's Okay to Not Know**: Leave technical sections blank if unsure - agent will figure it out
7. **Provide Context**: Background information helps the agent understand the full picture
8. **Dream Big**: Don't limit yourself - let the agent assess feasibility
