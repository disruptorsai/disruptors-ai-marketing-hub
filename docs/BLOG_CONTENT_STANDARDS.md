## Core Principles

### Content Quality Targets

- **Minimum Length:** ≥ 1,200 words
- **Format:** Complete long-form blog post in Markdown
- **Voice:** Narrative, skimmable, conversational
- **Audience:** Non-experts requiring step-by-step explanations
- **Reading Level:** Plain English with brief jargon definitions
- **First Impression:** Strong hook in opening (story, problem, myth vs. reality, or quick scenario)

### Brand Alignment

All content must align with Disruptors AI's brand voice:
- Bold and attention-grabbing
- Occasionally contrarian (challenges conventional wisdom)
- No fluff - every sentence adds value
- Built to engage and provoke thought
- Targeted at skilled trades and service businesses

## Article Structure

### Required Heading Hierarchy

1. **H1:** Single title that naturally includes primary keyword
2. **H2/H3:** Sections and subsections for content organization
3. **Natural headings:** Avoid generic terms like "Introduction" or "Conclusion"

### Opening Strategy

**First 150 Words Must Include:**
- One strong hook (story, problem, myth vs. reality, high-stakes scenario, or data snapshot)
- Primary keyword mentioned naturally
- Clear value proposition for reading

**Hook Patterns (Choose One):**
- Curiosity gap
- Vivid micro-story
- Contrarian myth-bust
- High-stakes scenario
- Data snapshot

### Paragraph Structure

**Guidelines:**
- **Standard length:** 2-4 sentences
- **Rhythm variation:** Occasional shorter lines for impact
- **Sentence variety:** Mix lengths to maintain engagement
- **Whitespace:** Use spacing to enhance readability

**Example Good Paragraph:**
```markdown
AI marketing tools can transform your business overnight. But only if you know how to use them correctly. Most companies make three critical mistakes that waste thousands in ad spend.

Here's how to avoid them.
```

## Style Guidelines

### Writing Voice

- **Tone:** Conversational and narrative
- **Person:** No first-person unless specified
- **Authority:** Confident without being condescending
- **Engagement:** Questions, scenarios, and relatable examples

### Emphasis & Formatting

**Use Sparingly:**
- **Bold** for key ideas and important terms
- *Italics* for emphasis
- Avoid over-formatting that clutters the page

**Forbidden Elements:**
- ❌ No em dashes (use commas or parentheses instead)
- ❌ Maximum two lists total per article
- ❌ No generic headings ("Introduction", "Conclusion", "Summary")
- ❌ No author voice unless explicitly requested
- ❌ No excessive formatting or decoration

### Language Requirements

- Plain English throughout
- Define any jargon briefly on first use
- Avoid industry buzzwords without context
- Short sentences for complex concepts
- Active voice preferred over passive

## Lists & Visual Cues

### List Constraints

**Critical Rule:** No more than TWO lists total in the entire article

**When to Use Lists:**
- Complex processes requiring step-by-step breakdown
- Key features or benefits comparison
- Checklist-style actionable items

**List Types:**
- Bulleted lists: 3-7 items maximum
- Numbered lists: For sequential processes
- Short, scannable items (1-2 sentences each)

### Visual Elements

**Blockquotes:**
- Optional: 1 short blockquote for key takeaway or expert quote
- Use for pull quotes or emphasis
- Keep concise and impactful

**Tables:**
- Avoid unless truly necessary
- Never put long sentences in tables
- Use for data comparison only

**Images:**
- Featured image required (generated via AI)
- In-content images optional
- Always include descriptive alt text

## Links Strategy

### Internal Links (1-2 per article)

**Requirements:**
- Link to relevant internal pages
- Use descriptive anchor text (not "click here")
- Place contextually where helpful to readers

**Format:**
```markdown
Learn more about [AI-powered marketing strategies](https://example.com/internal)
```

### External Links (1-2 per article)

**Requirements:**
- Link to high-authority, non-competitor sources
- Support key claims with credible references
- Use neutral, descriptive anchor text

**Best Practices:**
- Link to industry research, studies, or established authorities
- Avoid competitor websites
- Ensure links are current and functional

**Format:**
```markdown
According to [HubSpot's 2025 Marketing Report](https://example.com/external), businesses using AI see 30% higher ROI.
```

## FAQ Requirements

### Mandatory FAQ Section

**Requirements:**
- Exactly 5 FAQ questions and answers
- Use `###` heading for each question
- Concise, direct answers
- Based on real user search intent for the topic

### FAQ Structure

```markdown
### What is [topic] and why does it matter?

Brief, direct answer in 2-3 sentences that addresses the core question.

### How do I get started with [topic]?

Step-by-step guidance or clear entry point for beginners.

### What are the most common mistakes with [topic]?

Identify 3-4 key pitfalls to avoid with brief explanations.

### How long does [topic] take to show results?

Realistic timeline with conditional factors mentioned.

### What tools or resources do I need for [topic]?

Specific recommendations with brief justification.
```

### FAQ Ending

After FAQ section, include a **short CTA line:**
- "Explore our resources"
- "Get started today"
- "Ready to transform your marketing?"
- Keep to 1 sentence

## Output Rules

### Format Requirements

**Markdown Only:**
- Output complete Markdown without code fences/backticks
- Use proper heading levels (H1, H2, H3)
- Include proper line breaks between sections

**Forbidden Wrappers:**
```
❌ Wrong:
```markdown
# Title
Content here
```

✅ Correct:
# Title
Content here
```

### Content Organization

**Self-Contained Article:**
- Complete, ready-to-publish content
- No meta commentary or prefaces
- No placeholders or TODOs
- No editor notes or instructions

**Natural Flow:**
- Logical progression from section to section
- Smooth transitions between ideas
- Clear hierarchy of information
- Conclusion that ties back to opening hook

## SEO Optimization

### Keyword Integration

**Primary Keyword:**
- Appears in H1 title (naturally, not forced)
- Mentioned in first 150 words
- Distributed naturally throughout content (2-3% density)
- Used in at least one H2 heading

**Secondary Keywords:**
- Support primary keyword with semantic variations
- Include in subheadings where natural
- Use in body content for topical depth

### Meta Elements

**Title Optimization:**
- Include primary keyword
- 50-60 characters for SERP display
- Compelling and click-worthy

**Meta Description:**
- 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword
- Clear value proposition
- Call to action

### Content Structure for SEO

**Best Practices:**
- Clear heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
- Short paragraphs for scannability
- Strategic use of bold for key terms
- Internal linking to related content
- External links to authoritative sources

## Examples

### Example Opening (Good)

```markdown
# How AI-Powered Email Marketing Transforms Customer Engagement

Most businesses are leaving money on the table with their email campaigns. They send the same generic messages to everyone, hoping something sticks. Meanwhile, their competitors are using AI to personalize every interaction, increasing open rates by 40% and conversions by 28%.

The difference comes down to three critical strategies that AI-powered email marketing makes possible. These aren't theoretical concepts or future predictions. They're proven tactics that service businesses are using right now to build stronger customer relationships and drive measurable revenue growth.

Here's exactly how to implement them in your business.
```

**Why This Works:**
- Strong hook (myth-busting generic emails)
- Primary keyword in H1 naturally
- Keyword mentioned again in first 150 words
- Data-driven opening (40%, 28%)
- Clear value proposition
- No "Introduction" heading
- Conversational, engaging tone

### Example FAQ Section (Good)

```markdown
### What makes AI email marketing different from traditional email campaigns?

AI email marketing analyzes individual subscriber behavior to personalize content, timing, and offers automatically. Traditional campaigns send identical messages to everyone, while AI adapts each email based on past engagement, purchase history, and predicted interests.

### How long does it take to see results from AI-powered email marketing?

Most businesses see noticeable improvements in open rates within 2-3 weeks as the AI learns subscriber patterns. Conversion rate increases typically appear within 30-45 days once personalization strategies are fully implemented and optimized.

### Do I need technical expertise to use AI email marketing tools?

No. Modern AI email platforms are designed for non-technical users with drag-and-drop interfaces and pre-built templates. Setup typically takes 1-2 hours, and most platforms offer onboarding support to help you get started quickly.

### What's the cost difference between AI and traditional email marketing?

AI email platforms typically cost $50-300/month depending on subscriber count, compared to $10-50/month for basic email services. However, the 20-40% increase in conversions usually generates 3-5x ROI within the first quarter.

### Which AI email marketing platform should I choose?

Start with platforms that integrate with your current CRM and offer industry-specific templates. Top choices for service businesses include Mailchimp's Customer Journey Builder, HubSpot's AI Email Writer, and ActiveCampaign's predictive sending features.

Ready to transform your email marketing with AI? Explore our comprehensive guide to get started.
```

**Why This Works:**
- Exactly 5 questions using `###` headings
- Concise, direct answers (2-3 sentences)
- Addresses real user search intent
- Practical, actionable information
- Short CTA line at the end
