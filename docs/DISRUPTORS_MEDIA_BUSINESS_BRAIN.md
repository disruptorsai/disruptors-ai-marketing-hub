## 14. Voice & Style Guidelines

### Writing Tone

- **Professional but Conversational**: Sound like a knowledgeable friend, not a corporate robot
- **Confident but Humble**: Share expertise without arrogance
- **Clear over Clever**: Favor clarity and simplicity over jargon
- **Educational**: Teach as you sell
- **Human**: Use contractions, casual language when appropriate

### Vocabulary Preferences

**Use:**
- AI automation (not "machine learning algorithms")
- Marketing partner (not "vendor")
- Results (not "deliverables")
- Strategy (not "tactics" alone)
- Growth (not "scaling" excessively)

**Avoid:**
- Buzzwords: "synergy," "disruption," "game-changing" (unless ironic)
- Excessive jargon: "CTR optimization paradigm shift"
- Hyperbole: "guaranteed," "best ever," "revolutionary"
- Corporate-speak: "leverage," "utilize," "value-add"

### Content Examples

**Good Example (Brand Voice)**:
> "Most agencies treat AI like a magic wand—just wave it over your marketing and watch results appear. That's nonsense. AI is a tool, like a power drill. In the right hands with the right strategy, it's incredibly powerful. In the wrong hands, you just make expensive holes in the wrong places."

**Bad Example (Off-Brand)**:
> "Leverage our cutting-edge AI-powered marketing automation paradigm to synergize your digital transformation and unlock unprecedented ROI through revolutionary machine learning algorithms."
