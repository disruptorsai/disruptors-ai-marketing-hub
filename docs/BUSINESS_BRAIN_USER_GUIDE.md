# Business Brain User Guide

## What is the Business Brain?

The **Business Brain** is your company's AI-powered knowledge base that learns everything about your business to provide personalized AI-generated content. Think of it as your business's digital twin that understands your brand, products, customers, and unique value proposition.

### Why It's Game-Changing

**Traditional AI tools** generate generic content:
- No understanding of YOUR business
- No knowledge of YOUR customers
- No awareness of YOUR brand voice
- Requires manual customization every time

**Business Brain** generates personalized content:
- ✅ Knows your products/services
- ✅ Understands your ideal customers
- ✅ Maintains your brand voice
- ✅ Uses your target keywords
- ✅ Applies your unique positioning
- ✅ Gets smarter with every use

**This is the competitive advantage no one else offers yet.**

### Step 3: Start Using AI Tools

Once onboarding is complete:
- Your Business Brain is available in all apps
- All AI content is personalized to YOUR business
- Access Business Brain Manager to view/edit your brain

## Business Brain Levels

Your Business Brain evolves through 3 levels as it learns more about your business:

### Level 1: Starter Brain

**What it knows:**
- Basic business information (name, website, industry)
- Information from automatic website scraping
- Data you provided during onboarding
- 20-50 facts about your business

**Good for:**
- Getting started quickly
- Basic content generation
- Testing AI tools
- Understanding the system

**How to upgrade:**
- Add more facts manually
- Complete AI onboarding conversation
- Connect integrations
- Upload business documents

### Level 2: Enhanced Brain
**Confidence Score: 60-80%**

**What it knows:**
- Everything from Starter Brain
- Detailed product/service information
- Customer pain points and solutions
- Brand voice and tone guidelines
- Content strategy preferences
- Competitive differentiators

**Good for:**
- Professional content creation
- Brand-consistent messaging
- SEO-optimized content
- Social media posts
- Email campaigns

**How to upgrade:**
- Upload brand guidelines
- Add case studies and testimonials
- Configure detailed brand DNA
- Complete advanced AI training
- Connect more data sources

**Confidence Score: 90-100%**

**What it knows:**
- Everything from Enhanced Brain
- Complete brand guidelines (voice, tone, style)
- Visual identity (logos, colors, typography)

**Good for:**
- Enterprise-level content
- Multi-channel campaigns

- Highly personalized content

**How to achieve:**
- Upload comprehensive brand guidelines
- Complete full AI onboarding
- Add 100+ high-quality facts
- Configure all brand DNA settings
- Connect all available integrations
- Regular maintenance and updates

## Using the Business Brain Manager

### Accessing the Dashboard

1. Go to [Resources Page](https://dm4.wjwelsh.com/resources)
2. Click **Business Brain Manager**
3. If not logged in, you'll see the login modal
4. After login, your Business Brain dashboard loads

### Dashboard Overview

**Quick Stats:**
- **Brain Level**: Current level (Starter/Enhanced/Expert)
- **Confidence Score**: How much your brain knows (0-100%)
- **Total Facts**: Number of facts learned
- **Last Updated**: When brain was last enhanced

**Quick Actions:**
- Add Facts
- Upload Documents
- Scrape Website
- Configure Brand DNA
- View Training History

### Managing Facts

**View Facts:**
- Browse all facts your brain knows
- Filter by category (service, product, testimonial, etc.)
- Search by keyword
- Sort by confidence or importance

**Add Facts:**
```
Fact Type: Service
Category: Core Offering
Fact: "We specialize in custom WordPress development
       with a focus on performance optimization and
       SEO best practices."
Source: Manual Entry
Confidence: High
```

**Edit Facts:**
- Update fact text
- Change categorization
- Adjust confidence level
- Add source URL
- Mark as verified

**Delete Facts:**
- Remove outdated information
- Clean up duplicates
- Archive deprecated services

### Training Your Brain

**Manual Entry:**
- Add facts one by one
- Provide detailed information
- Categorize properly
- Set importance level

**Document Upload:**
- Upload brand guidelines (PDF)
- Upload marketing materials
- Upload case studies
- Upload company presentations

**Website Scraping:**
- Enter your website URL
- AI automatically extracts facts
- 20-50 facts typically found
- Can re-scrape for updates

**AI Conversation:**
- Chat with AI about your business
- AI asks questions to learn more
- Natural conversation format
- Extracts knowledge automatically

### Configuring Brand DNA

**Visual Identity:**
- **Primary Color**: Main brand color (#hex)
- **Secondary Color**: Accent brand color
- **Logo URLs**: Upload or link to logos
- **Typography**: Font families and styles
- **Design Style**: Modern, traditional, minimalist, bold

**Brand Voice:**
- **Voice Attributes**: Professional, friendly, authoritative, technical
- **Tone**: Formal, casual, conversational, academic
- **Writing Style**: Storytelling, technical, educational, promotional
- **Vocabulary Level**: Simple, intermediate, advanced, technical

**Content Strategy:**
- **Content Pillars**: Main topic categories (e.g., SEO, Web Design, Marketing)
- **Content Formats**: Blog, video, infographic, podcast, social
- **Publishing Frequency**: Daily, weekly, monthly
- **Seasonal Campaigns**: Holiday promotions, annual events

## Best Practices

### Fact Quality

**Good Facts:**
- Specific and detailed
- Source-backed (URL or document)
- Up-to-date and current
- Categorized correctly
- High confidence level

**Example Good Fact:**
```
Type: Service
Category: Core Offering
Fact: "Our WordPress Performance Optimization service
      reduces page load time by 40-60% through lazy loading,
      image compression, CDN integration, and database optimization.
      Average project completion time: 2-3 weeks."
Source: https://yoursite.com/services/wordpress-optimization
Confidence: High
```

**Poor Facts:**
- Vague or generic
- No source provided
- Outdated information
- Miscategorized
- Low confidence

**Example Poor Fact:**
```
Type: Other
Category: Custom
Fact: "We do good work"
Source: None
Confidence: Low
```

## Troubleshooting

### My confidence score is low

**Causes:**
- Not enough facts (minimum 20 recommended)
- Many low-confidence facts
- Missing key business information
- No brand DNA configured

**Solutions:**
1. Add more detailed facts
2. Upload brand guidelines
3. Complete AI onboarding conversation
4. Scrape your website
5. Verify existing facts

### AI content doesn't sound like my brand

**Causes:**
- Brand voice not configured
- Tone attributes not set
- Writing style undefined
- Insufficient training data

**Solutions:**
1. Configure Brand DNA in Business Brain Manager
2. Add examples of your preferred content style
3. Upload existing marketing materials
4. Set specific voice and tone attributes
5. Add facts about your brand personality

### Website scraping didn't find much

**Causes:**
- Website is behind a login
- Content is heavily JavaScript-based
- Website blocks scrapers
- Very new website with minimal content

**Solutions:**
1. Manually add facts about your business
2. Upload PDF documents instead
3. Use AI conversation to train your brain
4. Add facts from your knowledge base

### Can't find a fact I know I added

**Debugging:**
1. Use search function in Business Brain Manager
2. Check filters (type, category, confidence)
3. Look in "All Facts" view
4. Verify fact wasn't accidentally deleted
5. Check if fact is marked as deprecated

## FAQ

**Q: How long does it take to set up my Business Brain?**
A: Basic setup takes 5-10 minutes during onboarding. Reaching Expert level can take 1-4 hours depending on how much information you provide.

**Q: Can I have multiple Business Brains?**
A: Currently, one brain per user account. Multi-business support planned for future updates.

**Q: Will my brain learn automatically as I use AI tools?**
A: Yes! Facts used in successful content generation are marked as valuable and confidence scores improve over time.

**Q: Can I export my Business Brain?**
A: Yes, you can export all facts as JSON or CSV from Business Brain Manager.

**Q: What happens if I delete a fact?**
A: Fact is soft-deleted (archived) for 30 days, then permanently removed. You can restore during the 30-day window.

**Q: How do I invite team members?**
A: Team collaboration features are coming soon. Currently, one user per business brain.

**Q: Can I connect my CRM or other tools?**
A: Integrations are planned! Coming soon: HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Analytics, and more.
